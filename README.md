# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard – Excel

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing sales performance using Microsoft Excel. The dashboard presents key performance indicators (KPIs), regional and product-wise revenue analysis, and daily sales trends to generate meaningful business insights.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Compare revenue across different regions and products.
- Track daily revenue trends.
- Calculate important KPIs to understand business performance.
- Provide actionable business insights and recommendations.

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Charts and Data Visualization
- KPI Cards
- Data Analysis and Business Insights

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators
- **Total Revenue:** ₹11,91,600
- **Total Orders:** 400
- **Conversion Rate:** 10.60%
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** ₹2,979
- **Total Quantity:** 94
- **Total Visits:** 3,775

## 🔍 Key Business Insights
- Mumbai generated the highest regional revenue of ₹3,19,600.
- Laptop was the top-performing product, generating ₹4,22,500 in revenue.
- The overall conversion rate was 10.60%.
- September 1 recorded the highest daily revenue of ₹1,22,000, while September 7 recorded the lowest at ₹7,500.

## 💡 Recommendations
- Maintain strong sales performance in Mumbai.
- Analyze successful laptop sales strategies and consider applying them to other products.
- Investigate low-revenue days to identify possible sales or customer-traffic issues.
- Improve conversion rates through better offers, promotions, and customer engagement.
